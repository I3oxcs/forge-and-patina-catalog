In the summer of 1971, three privately entered Porsches arrived at Le Mans on wheels
forged by a small Cologne workshop — lighter than anything in production, with a profile
drawn for the circuit.

The original forging dies were lost for thirty years. We found the drawings in a storage
unit outside Stuttgart.

The Halden 71 is what they built.
