By 1964 the cars at the Nürburgring had changed. They were driven to the Eifel on
Friday, raced on Saturday and driven home again on Sunday — on the same set of wheels.

Our workshop drew a wheel for them: five broad spokes forged from a single billet, light
enough for the Nordschleife and strong enough for the autobahn home. It was the first
wheel we ever sold for the road.

The Eifel 64 is still the simplest thing we make.
